Privacy

Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Return Home Contact Us

01. Who we are

This site, drmorgan.ai, is published by Wopara Group. It presents the work of Dr. Ebere Morgan, Ph.D., founder of LIFT Global, Inc. (Leadership Impact For Transformation), in the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex, Texas, USA.

For any question about this policy or your information, write to drmorgan@drmorgan.ai.

02. What we collect

Information you send us. When you submit the engagement form we receive your name, organization, role, email address, selected area of interest, nature of inquiry, and the message you write. You choose what to put in these fields.

Questions asked of the site assistant. The assistant sends your typed question, together with extracts of this site's own published pages, to our processor so an answer can be generated.

Technical information. Our hosting and network providers record standard request data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used to serve the site and protect it from abuse.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers, we do not buy or sell personal information, and we do not build behavioural profiles of visitors.

03. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

If you switch between the light and dark appearance, that single preference is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It stays on your device, is not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

04. Why we use it, and on what basis

We use what you send solely to read, consider and respond to your inquiry, and to keep a record of the correspondence.

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, our lawful basis is your consent when you choose to submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about the practice and in keeping this site secure.

05. Who processes it for us

We keep the number of third parties deliberately small. Each is a processor acting on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare — runs the small service that receives the form and relays assistant questions, and protects the site from abuse.
  • Brevo — delivers the inquiry to our inbox as an email.
  • DeepSeek — generates the site assistant's answers from the question and the site extracts supplied with it.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the pages themselves.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash — serve the typefaces and the photography, and will therefore see your IP address as your browser requests those files.

Some of these providers operate outside your country, including in the United States. Where transfers are covered by the UK or EU GDPR, they are made under the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

Please do not put confidential or sensitive information into the assistant. Use the engagement form or email for anything of that nature.

06. How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with the matter it concerns, and afterwards where we have a legitimate or legal reason to retain it. When neither applies we delete it.

Assistant questions are not stored by us in any account or profile. Our providers may retain transmission logs for their own limited operational periods.

07. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to our use of it, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time.

If you are in California, you may also request disclosure of the categories of personal information collected and ask us to delete it. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

Write to drmorgan@drmorgan.ai and we will respond. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

08. Children

This site is intended for professional and institutional audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

09. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update it here and revise the effective date below.

Effective 4 August 2026. We will post any material change on this page and update the date above.
